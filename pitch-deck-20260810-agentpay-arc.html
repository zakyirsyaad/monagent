Product model

The funded balance
is the budget.

The user funds a Circle Agent Wallet once. The agent may spend that USDC balance on the requested workflow.

Safety comes from validation, idempotency, one-attempt mutations, receipt reconciliation, and withdrawal — not repeated payment approvals.
